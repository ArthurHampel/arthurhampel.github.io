The Domino Theory

My theory on the meaning of life.

1. Do something great enough to move humanity toward an age of abundance, and great enough that it inspires someone else. 2. That person goes on to do something great, which inspires others in turn. 3. One action becomes a chain reaction. 4. What you do today could still be lifting humanity millions of years from now.
